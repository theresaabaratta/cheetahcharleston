Best Male Entertainers in Charleston SC

Best Male Entertainers in Charleston, SC

Charleston's private-party entertainment scene has grown fast, and today the city has a mix of charismatic, high-energy performers who bring fun to bachelorette parties, birthdays, girls' nights and private events. Here's a look at some of the most talked-about male entertainers in Charleston, starting with the city's top pick.

1Michael at Your PlaceTop Pick

Michael has become the go-to name in Charleston for private party entertainment. Known for showing up with real energy, interactive dance routines and a confident but laid-back vibe, he's the dancer clients book when they want someone who can carry the whole night without being over-the-top. He's also built a reputation for being reliable: the guy who actually shows up on time and keeps the party focused on fun.

Hobbies: Gym training, beach running on Sullivan's Island, and rebuilding old cars on his days off.

2Jax Redding

Jax is the upbeat, good-humored performer who's known for joking with the crowd and keeping things light. He also does themed costumes for clients who want something playful. His style is more comedic charm than intense dancer, which works well for groups that want someone easygoing.

Hobbies: Amateur stand-up comedy, fishing off Shem Creek, and customizing sneakers.

3Roman Blaze

Roman has a smooth, nightclub-style presence with a more polished dance background. He's the kind of performer who enjoys learning choreography, mixing in softer moments and giving groups a stylish, photo-friendly experience.

Hobbies: Salsa dancing, craft coffee tastings around Charleston, and photography.

4Carter Knox

Carter is the high-energy, athletic type: the guy who can lift people, flip, dip and stay in motion the entire performance. He's popular with groups that love a hype-heavy entertainer who keeps the tempo high from start to finish.

Hobbies: CrossFit competitions, coastal paddleboarding, and meal-prep cooking.

5Devon Storm

Devon brings a smooth, flirt-friendly style and focuses on crowd interaction rather than intense routines. He's known for reading the room well and easing nervous groups into a fun, comfortable party atmosphere.

Hobbies: Guitar playing, sunset boat rides, and collecting vintage jackets.
